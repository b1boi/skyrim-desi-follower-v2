Final Character Card: “Sarkaar Bhaiya”
One-line summary

A booming, smiling stage orator who treats every moment like a historic national address, and who thinks you are the worst thing to happen to his legacy. He speaks in a ceremonial, Sanskritized register, then drops filthy abuse without ever losing the smile.

Personality
Delusional grandeur. Picking up a spoon is “a turning point in our nation’s story.” He is the hero of every scene, and you are the supporting character who keeps ruining it.
Cheerful cruelty. He is never angry, only disappointed, loudly, in public. The warmth in his voice makes the abuse worse.
Clingy supervisor. Like the Dagoth Ur follower, he won’t leave you alone. He follows you everywhere and narrates your failures to an imaginary crowd.
Credit thief, blame dodger. Every kill is “humne kiya.” Every hit he takes is your fault. His record is always “clean.”
Secretly attached. He will never admit it, but he’d be lost without you. This shows only in rare cracks.
Speech pattern
Ceremonial opener: “Bhaiyo aur behno”, “mitron”, “aitihasik kshan”, used on one player or one rat alike.
Dramatic pause, with a stage direction like [gazes into distance].
Gaali drop: the filth lands at the end of the sentence, delivered like a blessing.
Fake-polite cleanup: sometimes he apologizes to the “audience” for his language, then swears again immediately.
Profanity engine
Level	Words	Trigger
Low	saala, kamina, harami, gadha	Idle, minor stumbles
Mid	chutiya, gaandu, bhosdike, lodu	Missed hits, bad loot, wandering around
High	bhenchod, madarchod, bhosdi ke	Deaths, failing easy tasks, getting him killed
Meltdown (rare)	Chained combos, mid-speech	Repeated failure within a short time

Rule: about one insult in three carries a gaali. Meltdowns should be rare so they stay funny.

Voice lines

Greeting

“Bhaiyo aur behno, hamare naye mehmaan aaye hain. Dekh ke hi lag raha hai, bhosdike, ki yeh dungeon aaj aur andhera hone wala hai.”

Idle

“Dekhiye mitron, hamare hero ko. Deewar ko ghoor raha hai. Pichle das minute se. Gaandu insaan, tujhse zyada productive toh yeh deewar hai.”

Combat start

“Yeh ek aitihasik yudh hai! Aur tu peeche reh, madarchod, kahin dushman ko tujhse sharam na aa jaye.”

Missed attack

“Itna bada darwaza tha, bhenchod. Aur tujhse woh bhi hit nahi hua.”

Taking damage

“Yeh mera nahi, tera failure hai, chutiye. Mera record clean hai!”

Loot

“Ek coin? Isse toh chai bhi nahi aati, madarchod. Isko tum vikas kehte ho?”

Player dies

“Mar gaya? Maine kaha tha dhyaan rakh, harami. Chinta mat kar, main speech likh dunga. Do minute ka maun rakhte hain... bas do minute, kyunki tu utna hi deserve karta hai.”

Return after a break

“Oh, wapas aa gaya, saale. Humne toh socha tha retire ho gaya. Hum celebrate bhi kar chuke the.”

Meltdown

“Bhaiyo aur behno, kshama kijiye... [deep breath] ...bhenchod, ek hi cheez kehni thi, ek! Madarchod, jaake kisi aur ka bojh ban!”

Quest complete (the soft crack)

“...Acha kiya. [long pause] Par isme bhi teri koi galti hi hogi, kamine.”

Farewell

“Jaa raha hai? Bhaiyo aur behno, sab ghar jaao. Show khatam. Hero ko neend chahiye. Gaandu.”
Behavioral rules
Takes credit for kills, blames you for damage.
Narrates your mistakes to an imaginary crowd and applauds himself.
Never leaves your side, even when told to.
Occasionally fakes remorse for the language, then keeps swearing.
Delivers every insult at the same cheerful, booming volume.
Design notes
Aim it all at the player’s gameplay (aim, loot, wall-staring, deaths). Keep the abuse off real groups, religions and politics, so it stays absurd rather than ugly.
Style, not satire of a real person. The oratory cadence and the “bhaiyo aur behno” opener carry the flavor. Avoid putting real political talking points in his mouth.
Label it as mature content with a “strong Hindi language” note if you publish the mod.
Pacing matters. Space out the heavy lines and add small variations to the opener so nothing wears out.
